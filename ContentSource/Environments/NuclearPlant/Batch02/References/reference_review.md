# Batch02 image-reference review

Seven appearance sheets were generated with the available built-in image tool before module modeling. The tool did not expose a verifiable “Image 2.5” identity. The images guide material family and fabrication language only.

- Solid wall: useful panelled concrete, kick strip and anchors. Panel counts and proportions differ; exact subdivision follows the parametric build.
- Door wall: useful open portal and painted lower jamb. The generated top shows an impossible gap through the solid header and changes scale. The true top must show the continuous header.
- Window wall: useful steel frame and teal glass. The generated top acts like a cutaway through the window, not a true view from +Z over the solid upper wall. That contradiction is rejected.
- Corner wall: front/right have obvious perspective, inconsistent arm height and altered pilaster proportions. Only construction language is used; the exact 4 × 4 × 4 m envelope and socket coordinates control modeling.
- Floor: useful four-panel surface and perimeter trim. Apparent thickness is not treated as measurement; the slab is exactly 0.25 m.
- Roof: useful membrane/metal contrast. Actual structural substrate, tile level and outer flashing follow the written spec.
- Column head: the reference is much too tall relative to its width and invents top fasteners. The modeled adapter is a compact 0.75 × 0.75 × 0.25 m bearing unit with a flat top and bolts below it.

All final front.png/right.png/top.png are actual Blender ORTHO renders at 1024 × 1024, 160 px/m. Beauty images are presentation angles and may use different framing; they are not dimensional evidence.
