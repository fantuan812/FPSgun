# v02 — pre-model corrective review

Independent first-model QA found three assembly classes; this revision addresses only that complete defect set.

1. Hidden bases of raised duct segments, flush crossings, branch plates, apron panels and terminal foot grout now extend to the existing lowest datum z=-0.05m. Top elevations and route-facing footprints are unchanged. On slabs higher than that datum the base is intentionally embedded, rather than left suspended.
2. Three interior receiving terminals now touch the actual canopy waist-screen front surfaces y=-52.84/-39.84, not the farther perimeter wall. Their X positions are shifted clear of the canopy side screens by at least0.23m.
3. The outer cloth follows a conservative front/rear webbing envelope with25mm clearance and local rounded relief over the unchanged old buckles. New ratchets are moved above the old ones and seated on the new cloth. Eyelet rings intersect the cloth by2mm; tie cords run outside the hem before ending in actual pallet deck slats. Original cargo geometry remains intact.

The frozen v01 references, numeric input and provenance are preserved. v01 model outputs are archived in History/v01 before the v02 freeze; root Assets/Regions/site target paths are absent during this freeze. New v02 front/right/top, hidden-structure and regional drawings precede model construction. No finished model render is used as design input.
