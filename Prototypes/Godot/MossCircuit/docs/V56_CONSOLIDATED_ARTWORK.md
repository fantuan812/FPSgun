## How to read this history

**V45 through V56 below are local development and validation milestones, not twelve historical public releases.** This snapshot presents their accepted cumulative artwork together on the actual previously published v44 base. It does not manufacture intermediate commits, catalogs or downloadable resource packs.

The linked feature notes are retained byte-for-byte from their accepted local checkpoints. Their statements that publication/history assembly was pending describe the time each note was finalized. Their previous/new-pack rollback references identify local test artifacts, not a promise that those intermediate versions exist in the public catalog. For the current package, use the [v56-consolidated restoration instructions](../../../../Deliveries/MossCircuit/v56-consolidated/READ_ME_v56-consolidated.txt), not an old milestone delivery label. A local restore result is separate from a remote publication result.

## What the current validation establishes

The latest accepted Warden source contains 1,035 production inputs; its separate source-and-test wrapper contains 1,067. Source/packed resource identity and the unchanged [14-route BUILD entry](BUILD_UI_VALIDATION.md) passed. The Warden observer passed **885 predicates with 65 original stills, six controlled setups and 65 post-intent guards** after the explicitly retained setup correction. The author agent reviewed all 65 accepted originals; a separate QA agent reviewed 16. These are bounded automated/AI-agent observations, not human playtesting.

Earlier native observers remain evidence for their specific milestones and sampled routes. They are not presented as a newly rerun cumulative full-game suite. Source/resource checks, registered art previews, controlled native stills, local restoration controls and actual publication are different kinds of evidence. Counts must not be added together and advertised as one current test run.

Across the pass, runtime crops, source order, padding, anchors, scale, existing sockets where applicable, timing and gameplay behavior remain fixed. New paint bounds do not replace live registration. Decorative sparks, roots, ports and cracks do not introduce hitboxes, attack origins, obstacles or spawn authority. Editing requires the family-specific guide: sheet layouts, source-order permutations, half-pixel versus integral margins and inherited metadata are not interchangeable.

## Failures and limits remain part of the record

The linked notes retain rejected art pilots, strict failures and authorized narrow recoveries. Examples include chain zero-length renderer normalization, fixture-only source/PNG observers, player clock/weapon setup corrections, the Flanker check-only timeout recovery, and Warden's preparatory contact-reaction contamination. The original failures are not relabeled as first-pass success, and these external-observer corrections are not gameplay fixes. Exact same-package generated-scene comparisons are scoped exceptions, not broad tolerance for arbitrary resource changes.

Actual-size effects, small eyes, late remnants and subtle gait can merge or become faint. Selected mirrors and reduced-flash samples are not full Cartesian coverage. Controlled AI clocks, positions, HP and post-intent guards are not a naturally played encounter. Frozen stills do not establish real-time cadence, every final main-loop tint, every weapon alignment or every platform/DPI configuration. The Warden blast probe's frozen hero/weapon/effect synchronization limit is explicitly retained in its note.

Historical local restoration controls verify their own artifacts and recorded rollback/executable preservation; they do not by themselves certify the separately assembled consolidated package, Windows execution, installation or CI. Current restoration instructions and their actual acceptance status remain the authority for the consolidated delivery. No additional Core artwork, broader combat matrix or global asset-completion claim is included here.
