## Failures remain failures

Earlier compact-card visibility, test-parser, narrow-shop overflow, teardown-leak, duplicate-callback and test-wrapper preflight failures remain recorded as failures. Corrected candidates were checked separately. No extra teardown wait, warning suppression, shortened identifying label or relaxed clipping tolerance was used to turn those failures into a pass.

A strict generated-scene comparison also stopped the first packaging sequence. A single, exact four-byte generated node-metadata difference was subsequently accepted only after byte-level and decoded-scene comparison. The remaining checks continued on that same unchanged package. This is not a blanket metadata exception or a claim that every phase of the original failed sequence passed.

- At 640, focus visibility is checked before wheel input; the subsequent image demonstrates the full icon-plus-label union after scrolling. It does not also claim visibility of a distant focused control.
- Oversized card tops and tails are reachable, not necessarily visible together. Initial module-status views do not cover every possible status variant.

- Equal Linux/Windows export data does not prove a Windows run. Local file restoration/rollback does not prove a new executable launch or remote publication.
