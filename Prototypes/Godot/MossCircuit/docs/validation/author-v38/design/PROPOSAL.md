# v38 proposal — Stitch Drone / 补缀蜂 (design only)

No production edits or target runs. Candidate for independent contract review, not an accepted implementation.

## Actual gap
The frozen v37 encounters contain seven kinds: four direct attackers (crawler, charger, spitter, flanker), Broodkeeper (annulus/summon), and two bosses. EnemyAI.tick receives only its own actor/player/time; no existing ally selection/healing exists. ## Player-facing bounded unit
One new ordinary enemy: Stitch Drone. A fragile support actor chooses ONE wounded ordinary ally, stops moving and visibly channels a repair. Any positive HP loss to the drone during that channel cancels it; all existing primary/proc damage works. Kill the target, kill/intercept the drone, or separate the tether to counter it. No immunity, armor, damage reduction, healing player, projectile reflection, weapon requirement or extra shop module.

Suggested initial parameters for review: HP 34, speed 30, contact damage 6, XP 7, scale 1.0, desired player distance 210; ally range 150 px; channel 1.2 s; cooldown 5 s; heal flat 10 HP, clamped to target maximum. Initial acquisition cooldown .8 s retains ordinary spawn convention. Failed acquisition consumes 1 s retry interval; completed/interrupted channel consumes the full 5 s. No heal-over-time or banked charges. Numeric tuning remains proposed until contract/oracle freeze.

Acquisition eligible: living, wounded, not self, not boss, not another Stitch Drone, and parent_id == -1 (summoned children excluded). Choose lowest HP/maxHP ratio, then nearest squared distance, then smallest stable enemy ID. Lock ID, never retarget mid-channel. Range tested at acquisition and completion on actual current clamped positions; equality at 150 is eligible. No random choice or additional RNG consumption.

## Precise ordering to freeze before implementation
- Use a separate small EnemySupport policy for target choice/eligibility/settlement, with shared CombatRuntime as sole world mutation owner; do not route healing through player RunFlow or alter damage math.
- Existing AI/movement and existing attacks retain their order. A support warning stores its own HP at channel start and target ID, not a target Dictionary alias. Support actors cannot receive support healing, so HP decrease is an unambiguous interrupt without refactoring every primary/proc damage path.
- Observe interrupt/invalid target each tick; if a completion is due, queue a support intent, do not heal inside the enemy loop. Resolve at the END of the existing combat step, after player projectiles, death/kill procs and pickup procs, before final focus pruning. Require playing, both actors still present and HP > 0, target still eligible and within range, and drone HP >= its channel-start HP. Thus a lethal projectile/kill-chain/pickup proc earlier in this step wins over repair. Never resurrect or undo a death/drop.
- Damage before channel acquisition is not retrospectively an interrupt: snapshot is taken at actual channel entry. Damage after entry, including the same step's late projectile/proc phases, cancels. Zero or negated damage is not an interrupt. Existing runtime has no enemy healing, and new support excludes all support actors; if another future healing source is added this HP-snapshot assumption must be revisited.
- Process simultaneously due support intents by stable source ID. Each reevaluates current target HP; repair clamps and a now-full target wastes the pulse. No multiplication of max HP or permanent buffs. Explicitly test two drones targeting the same ordinary ally.
- No catch-up bursts: large dt permits at most one pulse per drone per step. Newly spawned summoned children are excluded; newly spawned ordinary actors may only acquire/be selected when present at the defined acquisition phase. Freeze exact mode spawn-before-step behavior in the contract.
- Pauses/shop/upgrade do not advance channel; terminal/direct-step must not repair. Room clear, restart/title and run reset discard all channel state and tether markers. A dead drone's retained death sprite cannot emit a tether or repair.
- Bosses and all parent_id != -1 children excluded. Support actor itself may exist alongside bosses but never repairs them. No child inheritance of support ability. Distinguish same-kind separately spawned ordinary actors (allowed targets if not support) from summoned children (excluded).
