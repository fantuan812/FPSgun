## What changes

In a normal shop, retain an explicitly selected STANDARD owned weapon and consume the displayed lowest-index other STANDARD copy of the same ID. The survivor is TEMPERED for this run: one tier, ×1.6 nominal primary damage, one existing attack timer and one tag copy. The sacrificed copy frees a slot, grants no scrap/refund, and its lost tag can disable a threshold. Both participants must be standard; separate pairs can produce separate tempered copies, never a ladder.

This is not a guarantee of more damage than two originals. Two unmodified Rivets can deal 30 total primary damage in a controlled aligned single cycle; one tempered Rivet deals24. Fewer events can also weaken Echo, cold-mark application, healing opportunities or other existing effects. Actual encounter/target/travel timing still matters.

The survivor keeps its exact remaining cooldown, aim and recoil age; all other copies retain their state. Any outstanding player projectile or pending mortar disables fusion, rather than deleting/rebinding it. Coins, fractional credit, HP/dash, stock/reservation, upgrades, effects, hazards and RNG do not change at the click. Future affinity naturally reflects fewer owned copies/tags. Current offers are not rerolled.

The AFTER TEMPER preview uses a detached resulting build and the original player base. Three Rail copies currently activate 36 damage each; fusing leaves two tags and a 48-damage tempered survivor, not 57.6. Three Shotguns lose the tag's sixth pellet when fused to two copies. These are specific rule examples, not broad balance certification.

Owned shop/inventory cards expose STANDARD/TEMPERED and actual per-copy readouts. Replacement selectors identify slot/type/tier. Tempered resale is one ordinary catalog copy under the existing resale formula; no premium for consumed material. New purchases/replacements are standard, even the same ID. New runs and same/new-seed retries reset to standard starter builds. There is no account/save/unlock system.

## Bounded implementation

- Flat weapon IDs remain unchanged. Optional integer 0/1 `build.weapon_temper` appears only after the first successful fusion; never-used paths retain the complete old build schema.
- Every weapon alignment mutation maintains or strictly rejects the tier array. Invalid present metadata is never repaired silently; views/fire fail safe without a boost and mutation actions reject it. Existing direct-fire ID/slot mismatch remains standard for compatibility.
- A single catalog top-level profile defines tier 1 and 1.6. All old 8 weapon / 29 module records, prices, tags, weights, encounters, starting kits and art remain exact.
- Shared weapon damage computes old global/tag damage, then tier once, then the existing target brace. Arc remains 40% of the resolved initiating hit; fixed Fracture 16 / Echo 12 / Feedback 24 and other fixed effects remain fixed. Tier changes no weapon ID: tempered Cryo is not a different type for Fracture.
- Existing projectile/blast damage fields snapshot nominal tier damage at launch. Later hits do not read mutable tier/slot state. Existing ordinary replacement cleanup semantics are not changed.
- Pure `Rules.derive` updates only derived stats after fusion, preserving HP/dash, rather than invoking the wider effects/hazard/loadout cleanup path.
