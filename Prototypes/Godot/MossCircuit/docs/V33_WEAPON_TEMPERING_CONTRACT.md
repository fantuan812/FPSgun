## 1. One actual build decision

Current duplicates each consume one of four weapon slots and supply one attack timer/tag copy. There is no way to trade duplicate cadence for slot efficiency. Add shop-only **TEMPER**: retain the selected STANDARD owned weapon, permanently consume the lowest-index other STANDARD copy of the identical weapon ID, and turn the retained copy into one TEMPERED copy for this run.

- Exactly two standard copies become one tempered copy. Only tiers 0 (STANDARD) and 1 (TEMPERED) exist. A tempered copy cannot be either participant in a later fusion. With four standard copies, two independent tempered copies are possible; they cannot fuse together.
- Current authored tier multiplier is **1.6** on that copy's nominal primary damage. Its interval, reach, pellet count, pierce, status duration, geometry and behavior are unchanged except for consequences of losing an ordinary tag contribution. No universal DPS gain: one timer at 1.6 times damage is not two timers, and fewer primary-hit opportunities can weaken existing effects or cold-mark application.
- No scrap charge or credit, sale refund, pickup, upgrade choice, extra shop, reroll, new weapon/module row, art asset, RNG call, enemy, input binding, saved progress or system. The consumed copy is gone and frees one weapon slot. The retained copy contributes ONE ordinary weapon tag copy, not two and not a bonus tag.
- The top-level `weapon_tempering` profile in `data/catalog.json` is the single data source for `max_tier:1` and `primary_damage_multiplier:1.6`. Existing 8 weapon / 29 module rows, prices, weights, max-stacks, tags and all existing synergies stay exact. No per-weapon exceptions or tier ladder. Validation requires the profile object, finite numeric multiplier strictly between 1 and 2 (not bool/string/container), and integral numeric max_tier exactly 1; malformed data fails startup validation. Tests pin the authored current values as 1 / 1.6.

## 2. Minimal per-copy representation and lifecycle

- Retain the existing flat `build.weapons: Array[String]`; do not rewrite catalog IDs, projectile identity or loadout representation. Add optional `build.weapon_temper: Array` only when the first successful temper action occurs. Absent metadata means every copy is STANDARD; normal old-path factories, views, buys, sells, moves, retries and combat must NOT eagerly insert an all-zero array.
- Once present, metadata has exactly the weapon count and each entry is an actual integer 0 or 1. Booleans, numeric strings, floats (including 0.0/1.0), null, nested data, negative or greater tiers and length mismatch are invalid. Never silently repair or downgrade present invalid state.
- Present valid metadata follows the exact same weapon copy across move. Sale removes the corresponding entry. Weapon purchase appends 0, and replacement sets that slot to 0 even when replacing with the same ID. After first temper, an all-zero array may legitimately remain following a sale/replacement; do not infer a tier or grant a refund.
- Every existing alignment-changing seam rejects present invalid metadata before mutation: weapon purchase/replacement, weapon sale, move, temper. Nonweapon buy/sell, reserve/release/refresh and other old operations retain their existing eligibility; this is not a blanket transaction lock. Read-only/current damage access must fail safe without a tier boost or crash, expose invalid tier data in view/status, and never repair live state.
- Fresh run, all three starting loadouts, same/new-seed retries and a new process produce the existing all-standard factory build without tier metadata. Ordinary stage continuation and pause/inventory/settings retain copy tiers. No persistent mastery, future-purchase bonus or account/unlock state.

## 3. Exact guarded action and authority delta

- Intent payload contains retained `slot`, `donor_slot`, `generation`, `id`, `donor_id`. All indices/generation must be actual integers; IDs actual Strings. The two IDs must match the current two slots and each other. No coercion of bool/float/string/container/null.
- Require current shop state/generation, recognized 2–4 weapon copies within the existing cap, exact finite numeric cooldown-array length, valid optional tier metadata, distinct in-bounds slots, two standard copies, and donor equal to the lowest other standard matching-ID slot. Require BOTH player bullets and pending mortar blasts empty. Reject before lazy rig synchronization or any other mutation; outstanding shots are not deleted, rebound or altered.
- All invalid/stale/wrong-state/unavailable requests change nothing: authority, message, revision/generation, rig, RNG and effects remain exact. A retained callback cannot consume a newly shifted same-ID copy after any successful shop mutation.
- On success, materialize a detached all-zero tier array only if previously absent; mark the retained original slot tier 1 and remove exactly the donor weapon, tier and cooldown. Remaining weapons keep their relative order. The retained copy's final slot shifts down by one iff the donor was earlier.
- Keep the retained and every unrelated copy's exact remaining cooldown, including negative/zero ready clocks. Do not select a minimum, average, synchronize, normalize, decrement or reset cooldowns. Choosing a ready survivor is an explicit choice among genuinely ready copies, not a granted attack or free cooldown reset.
- Keep the retained and unrelated rig records, sprite identity, aim and recoil age. First synchronize against the PRE-action IDs only if already lazily mismatched; then remove/free the donor's record/sprite only, reindex the remaining slots and invalidate only poses whose mount index changed. Preserve visible rig clock/generation and historical last_shot diagnostics when pre-action rig was aligned. No new launch, fire event or animation reset.
- The rig must match post-removal IDs before the stat update. Reuse pure `Rules.derive(config.player, reduced_build, catalog)` exactly once while retaining current HP/dash fields exactly: electric/melee/spread thresholds can deactivate, affecting other weapons too. Do NOT invoke the full `_recompute` path, which also synchronizes effects and filters hazards; no effect synchronization or loadout-change shot/cache cleanup is needed for this weapons-only action. No heal, HP gain, dash/hurt reset, proc event, effect invalidation/epoch change, ICD/counter/charge/shield refund or hazard cleanup caused by this action.
- Increment `build.revision` once and `shop_generation` once; rerender shop and state retained/consumed slots and final tier in a clear success message. Existing buy/replace/sell/reserve/release/refresh/deploy and temper/move callbacks from the old generation are stale.
- Coins/fraction, upgrades, pending choices, held quote/token/price/metadata, existing offers/sold flags, RNG states, HP/dash/hurt, elapsed/encounter state, proc counters/ICDs/epochs, hazards/pickups and all art remain exact. Intended differences: one removed weapon/clock/rig row, one tier, resulting tags/derived damage or pellets, future weighted shop pool and its resulting draws because actual ownership changed, UI/readouts and future attacks.

## 4. Damage calculation, snapshots and secondary effects

- `Rules.weapon_damage` remains the single numeric calculation used by firing and current readout. Compute the old ordered global/tag weapon damage first, then multiply by the data-defined tier factor exactly once for a valid TEMPERED copy. STANDARD/default factor 1 follows the exact old operation path. No rounding, HP clamp, minimum damage or general stat.damage multiplier.
- Firing resolves the factor from this weapon's slot + matching ID, not all copies of its ID. A standard and tempered copy of the same type can coexist with different exact damage, clocks and rig records. Direct `WeaponCombat.fire(id, slot)` is also an existing test seam: absent/invalid metadata, out-of-range slot or ID mismatch grants no boost and preserves the old standard direct-fire behavior rather than rejecting legacy calls. Normal shared runtime already supplies matching slots. The same tier helper and current readout formula serve combat and UI; base/offer/starter readouts remain standard and clearly BASE.
- This nominal primary value enters existing v32 target brace resolution afterward. Arc Relay derives its existing 40% from the final brace-resolved hit event as before; it is not multiplied by tier a second time. Existing fixed Fracture 16, Echo 12, Feedback 24, fields, thorns and other fixed effects remain fixed. Hit-event count/depth/order, kill attribution, Cryo surviving-target/chill order and existing primary-only proc restrictions remain unchanged.
- Ordinary/spread/pierce/Return/Bankshot launches snapshot tier-modified nominal damage into the EXISTING shot damage field. Mortar snapshots it into the EXISTING pending-blast damage field. Each later target independently applies existing brace rules; later mutable copy slots/tier data are not consulted. Immediate Orbit/Cryo use the same shared nominal calculation at fire time. No projectile key/schema or contact-order change is needed.
- Exact no-temper-path old gameplay, transactions, timing and RNG projections must equal v32. Catalog's new top-level profile and opt-in view metadata are outside that projection; all old catalog records are identical. After actual fusion, no equivalent outcomes or cross-version seeded shop result promise is made: lost tags/copies intentionally change affinity, thresholds, hits and kills.

### Independent expected-value anchors (not yet executed)

At zero bonuses, STANDARD → TEMPERED nominal primary values are Rivet 15 → 24; Shotgun pellet 9 → 14.4; Rail 30 → 48; Saw 20 → 32; Return 18 → 28.8; Mortar 36 → 57.6; Cryo 9 → 14.4; Bankshot 18 → 28.8. A qualifying brace front then halves these values once; fixed secondary values remain unchanged. Three Rails currently have 36 each through ARC GRID; fusing to two tag copies leaves the retained tempered Rail at 48, NOT 57.6. Three Shotguns lose the threshold's extra pellet when fused to two copies (6 → 5 per attack). These are authored arithmetic expectations requiring actual-path tests, not completed validation or balance claims.

## 5. Economy and irreversible UI disclosure

- A tempered weapon sells for ONE ordinary catalog copy under the existing resale formula, including Recycler's old cap/floor rules. No tier premium, material recovery or refund for the consumed duplicate. Replacement still destroys the outgoing weapon without refund; replacement/new purchase is STANDARD.
- Each eligible owned shop card identifies the retained slot and named matching consumed slot. Button text explicitly says `TEMPER / CONSUME SLOT N`; adjacent visible short copy says irreversible/no refund, frees one slot and removes one copy/tag contribution. No action in inventory, pause, title, upgrade, result or combat. Tempering is explicit and never first/default activation; existing keyboard/mouse navigation remains usable.
- Show STANDARD / TEMPERED on owned shop and inventory weapon cards; tempered uses the existing weapon icon, not a fake new weapon asset. Current damage readouts are tier-aware; sale values remain honest. Full-slot replacement options identify slot and tier so the player knows which copy will be destroyed, including same-ID duplicates.
- Include a read-only **AFTER TEMPER** preview derived from a detached candidate build, `Rules.derive(config.player, detached_result_build, catalog)`, and shared weapon readout. Always use the original config.player base, never already-derived current stats (which would double-apply bonuses). It must reflect lost thresholds (electric/melee/spread), not simply multiply the pre-action card by 1.6. No live state, RNG, timers, rig or effects mutation from preview/render. Show relevant post-action damage/pellet readout and lost-tag/cadence warning; do not claim total DPS or universally +60% versus the prior current card.
- Lowest other standard matching donor is deterministic and explicitly displayed; choosing another retained card can select a different survivor. Once tempered, that card cannot temper again. If no standard donor exists or flight/state data blocks action, display a concise reason/disabled state instead of an unsafe active button.
- Revalidate actual 960×540 and 1280×720 in both modes: two/three/four duplicate cards, standard+tempered mix, full slots, long names/readouts, replacement controls, reserved offer, scroll/focus, keyboard activation, repeated/stale callbacks, deployment and current inventory. No new binding or physical-controller/audio/Windows claim.
